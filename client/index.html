Sort the food, match three, clear the grill

Grill Shuffle is a tactile grill puzzle game. Shrimp, steak, corn and drumsticks are scattered across sizzling grills: drag food between grills, put three of a kind together and watch them flame up and get served. Every level is a compact food puzzle with a move limit, and every one has been solved by a computer so you know a clean solution exists.

How to play

  1. Drag a food item (or tap it, then tap a grill) to move it into an empty slot.
  2. Three matching foods on one grill sizzle and clear.
  3. Clear consecutive matches for a combo.
  4. Finish the order before you run out of moves. Fewer moves earn more stars.

Daily puzzle and friend challenges

A new BBQ puzzle every day, the same board for everyone. Finish any level and share a challenge link: your friends open the exact same grill and try to beat your move count.
